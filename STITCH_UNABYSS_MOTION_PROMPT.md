# Stitch Unabyss Motion Prompt

Use this prompt when Stitch has the right data objects but the screen still feels too static, generic, or security-operations oriented.

```text
Keep the existing IP Corp Architecture Brain product scope, but redesign the visual language and motion direction.

I want the modern look and motion energy of https://unabyss.com/ as inspiration only. Do not copy Unabyss branding, copy, page structure, or assets.

Translate the reference into this product:
- A premium dark context workspace, not a generic admin dashboard.
- Layered depth, glassy but restrained surfaces, crisp edges, and high-contrast typography.
- Small source/status chips that make Teams, Cluely, Notion, project-memory, Natively, outcomes, and live captures feel organized.
- Motion that explains the product: source evidence flows into the brain, meetings assemble into prep packets, Cortex insights unfold their reasoning chain, and approval-gated actions move into review.
- Subtle animated freshness/readiness states, proof trails, and hover expansions for evidence refs.
- Missing evidence should be visible but not catastrophic.
- Proposed ADRs should feel like governance review, not a red emergency outage.

Do not invent operations telemetry. No active nodes, protocol alerts, server locations, throughput, latency, security protocols, or asset health.

Use the real data objects:
- Freshness: fresh-no-new-inputs-after-pryor-promotion
- Next best packet: weekly-fabric-post-onsite-synthesis
- Counts: 14 prep packets, 20 Cortex insights, 13 action proposals, 48 open questions, 22 risks, 6 proposed ADRs
- Missing evidence: May 27 plant-tour follow-up has no post-meeting capture yet
- Open proposals: proactive coaching disable-gate audit and Fabric calendar reset details
- Key review items: DQ-048 Pryor reporting path, ADR-0006 plant-floor evidence stream contract, brain stakeholder access boundary

Rename the harsh ops labels:
- "Priority Intervention Required" becomes "Needs Attention"
- "System Blockers" becomes "Decisions Needing Review"
- "Current Global State" becomes "Brain Snapshot"
- "Safe to Share: Yes" becomes "Stakeholder-safe draft"
- "+ New Decision" becomes "Capture Note" or "Draft ADR"

Design the first screen as a Brain Readiness Workspace. It should feel like a living context headquarters where stakeholders can see what is current, what needs review, what evidence supports claims, and what packet is ready for the next meeting.

Also fix responsive behavior. On tablet/mobile, the sidebar must collapse or become a bottom/sheet navigation so content never clips off the device.
```
