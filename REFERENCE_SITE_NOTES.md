# Reference Site Notes

Reference: https://unabyss.com/

Steve likes the design and style direction of Unabyss. Use it as inspiration only.

## What To Borrow Conceptually

- A strong product frame around context chaos becoming organized context.
- Multiple source systems represented as approachable chips, cards, or rails.
- Segmented context as a central product concept.
- Permission and access-level controls as first-class UI, not settings afterthoughts.
- Clear progression from source connection to usable context.
- Agent/MCP readiness as part of the product language.
- Confident, minimal copy with strong nouns and short explanations.

## What Not To Copy

- Brand name, copy, assets, or exact page layout.
- Any marketing-page structure that gets in the way of a usable stakeholder workspace.
- Public SaaS pricing/acquisition sections.

## IP Corp Translation

Raw engagement evidence comes in from meetings, project memory, Natively, Power BI analysis, risks, ADRs, and action proposals. The frontend turns that into a safe, segmented, stakeholder-ready context surface.
